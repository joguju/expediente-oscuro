Privacy Policy — Expediente Oscuro

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Expediente Oscuro is a personal tool used by a single individual (the developer) to upload the developer's own original videos to the developer's own TikTok account.

What we access. When the developer authorizes the app through TikTok Login Kit, the app receives an access token and the basic profile information (display name) of that single account, with the permissions video.upload and video.publish.

What we do with it. The token is used only to upload and publish videos created by the developer to that account. It is stored locally on the developer's own computer and is never shared, sold or transmitted to third parties.

What we do not do. The app does not collect data from other TikTok users, does not read private messages, and does not use data for advertising or profiling.

Retention and deletion. Tokens can be deleted at any time by deleting the local token file or by revoking the app's access in TikTok settings (Settings and privacy > Security > Manage app permissions).
